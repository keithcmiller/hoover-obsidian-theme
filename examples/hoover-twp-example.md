# Residential Framing — Product Specification Guide

Reference document for field reps and contractors sourcing treated lumber for residential construction applications.

## Specifying for Common Residential Scenarios

### Deck framing and ledger boards

Specify **UC4A** for posts in contact with the ground. Use **UC3B** for joists and beams above grade. Ledger boards attached to house framing should be **UC3B** minimum.

> [!warning]
> Ledger attachment to rim boards or engineered lumber requires corrosion-resistant hardware rated for treated wood contact. Standard zinc-coated fasteners are not sufficient.

### Sill plates

All sill plates in contact with concrete or masonry require **UC4B** minimum per IRC R317. Confirm with local AHJ whether **UC4C** is required in high-moisture jurisdictions.

### Fence posts

Ground-contact fence posts: **UC4A** for decorative fences, **UC4B** where structural performance matters (privacy fences over 6′, windbreak applications).

## Handling & Installation Notes

- Allow treated lumber to dry before painting or staining — typically 2–4 weeks after delivery depending on climate
- Use stainless steel, hot-dipped galvanized, or approved coated fasteners only
- Cuts and drilled holes should be field-treated with an approved end-cut preservative
- Dispose of treated wood scraps per local regulations — do not burn
