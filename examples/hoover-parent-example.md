# Q2 Contractor Outreach Initiative

A coordinated effort to strengthen relationships with regional building contractors ahead of the summer construction season.

## Overview

Building professionals across the Southeast are navigating increasingly complex material decisions under tighter timelines. This initiative positions Hoover as the trusted partner who removes friction at every step — from spec selection to job-site delivery.

---

## Objectives

- Increase qualified contractor accounts by 15% across the Gulf Coast region
- Deliver product education sessions to at least 40 contractor crews by end of Q2
- Reduce average order-to-delivery time to under 5 business days for stocked SKUs
