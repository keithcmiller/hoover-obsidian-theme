# Exterior Cladding — Architect Specification Reference

Guidance for architects and fabricators working with Hoover Architectural Solutions cladding products on commercial and institutional projects.

> [!has]
> Hoover Architectural Solutions products carry full EPD and Declare Label documentation. Request current EPD files from your Hoover rep before finalizing specifications for LEED or Living Building Challenge projects.

## Design Freedom Considerations

One of the primary motivations for specifying Hoover AS products is fabrication flexibility. The following capabilities are available across most product lines:

- **Complex geometry:** Curved, tapered, and angled panels can be fabricated to spec. Provide shop drawings or DXF files.
- **Perforation patterns:** Custom perforation available for solar shading and decorative applications. Minimum open area 15%, maximum 60%.
- **Integrated reveals:** Reveal joints of 3/8″ to 1″ are standard. Wider reveals available with engineering review.
- **Large-format panels:** Maximum panel size varies by product. Apex Panel: 60″ × 144″. Ridgeline: continuous run limited by shipping at 240″.

## Performance & Compliance

### Fire

> [!warning]
> Product fire ratings vary by substrate, attachment system, and assembly. Do not specify a fire classification without confirming the tested assembly. Hoover AS does not warrant fire performance of field-modified assemblies.

| Product | NFPA 285 | ASTM E84 Class A |
|---|---|---|
| Apex Panel (aluminum face) | Tested — confirm assembly | Yes |
| Ridgeline (solid aluminum) | Tested — confirm assembly | Yes |
| Cortex | Assembly-dependent | Yes |
| Terraform | Assembly-dependent | Yes |

### Sustainability

All Hoover Architectural Solutions products are:
- Tested to US and CAN standards
- EPD available on request
- Declare Label available on request
- Compatible with LEED v4.1 MR credits (confirm specific product)
